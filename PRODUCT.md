# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audiences are potential PhD supervisors and quantitative hiring managers. They visit to evaluate Malek M. Itani's research ability, technical rigor, intellectual interests, and demonstrated work across research, writing, and prototypes.

The preferred visitor action or conversion is still an open decision.

## Product Purpose

This is Malek M. Itani's professional portfolio. It brings together applied research, long-form writing, publications, and working prototypes so evaluators can inspect both the reasoning and the resulting work.

The site should make substantive work easy to discover, understand, and assess without flattening its uncertainty or complexity.

## Positioning

Open decision: Malek will revise the site's positioning later. Future work must not silently turn the current homepage tagline or descriptive copy into a permanent positioning statement.

## Operating Context

Visitors enter through a portfolio homepage and move among four principal areas:

- Blog posts and essays
- Research projects and publications
- Working prototypes
- Biography, experience, education, tools, and contact links

The site is a Quarto website intended for static publishing. Content is authored in QMD, with automatic project and post listings, RSS, search, social metadata, and page-level assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Quarto is the established publishing stack and repository structure.
- The site supports research pages, blog posts, prototype pages, automatic listings, and long-form interactive articles.
- Light and dark themes, responsive layouts, RSS, search, and social metadata are existing capabilities.
- Existing factual copy, biography, experience, education, publications, links, portrait, and other content should be treated as confirmed rather than placeholder material unless Malek later changes them.
- The current positioning is intentionally provisional.
- Preserve the distinction between research, writing, and prototypes while making their relationship legible.

## Brand Commitments

- The public name is Malek M. Itani.
- The existing site content and identity assets are authoritative unless Malek explicitly replaces them.
- The established voice is thoughtful, candid, technically literate, and evidence-conscious.
- Claims should remain specific and defensible; uncertainty should be communicated rather than concealed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Research project pages and a publication list are present under `research/`.
- Working prototype pages are present under `prototypes/`.
- Essays and interactive long-form writing are present under `blog/posts/`.
- Identity and portfolio assets are present under `assets/`.
- The About page contains confirmed biography, experience, education, tools, and contact information.
- The existing CV link is confirmed content; if its referenced file is unavailable during future work, do not fabricate or silently replace it.
- Do not invent testimonials, employers, institutional affiliations, metrics, awards, or publication status.

## Product Principles

1. Make rigor inspectable for PhD supervisors and quantitative hiring managers.
2. Show the reasoning behind the work, not only polished outcomes.
3. Connect research depth with evidence of practical building ability.
4. Preserve factual precision and clearly communicate uncertainty.
5. Help a serious evaluator reach the strongest evidence quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain a readable, keyboard-accessible web experience across desktop and mobile, with meaningful document structure, image alternatives, sufficient contrast, dark-mode support, and reduced-motion behavior for interactive work.
